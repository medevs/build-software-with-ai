   <h2>"I can't reproduce it" is a real problem, not an excuse</h2>
   <p>If a bug can't be reliably triggered, it can't be reliably confirmed as fixed either. "It seems fine now" after a guessed-at fix is not the same as "we found the actual cause and corrected it." The bug may simply be waiting for the same rare conditions to line up again.</p>
   <p>This is exactly why <a href="11-testing-basics.html">tests</a> matter: a bug that gets a test written for it can never silently come back unnoticed. The test becomes a permanent, automatic check that this specific mistake stays fixed.</p>
