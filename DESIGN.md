# Design System: AI Builder Academy

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Data Sheet"**

This is a Geigy-Basel product monograph (1953–72) rendered as static HTML: white
stock, printing-ink hairlines, tight Helvetica caps for every piece of furniture,
and a serif — Charter — reserved for running text. It is aimed at a professional
with four spare minutes, and the ambition is that the sheet is precise enough and
well set enough that people keep it. Diagrams are not illustrations dropped into
prose; they are the architecture, and every one is a numbered plate.

The world is deliberately flat. There is no radius anywhere in the two
stylesheets and no blurred shadow anywhere in the two stylesheets. Depth is a
ruled line: a 3px heavy rule opens a block, a 1px ink hairline separates the
things inside it, a 1px soft hairline separates the rows within those. Colour
arrives at page scale — a module's ink fills a whole panel and carries white type
— never as a small tinted chip. The one authored moment of motion is a flat
colour field wiping down the way a press lays ink; the text is on screen from the
first frame regardless.

Two things are explicitly refused. The modern documentation site: no sidebar
tree, no thin sans, no blue links. And the site's own former identity: no cream
paper, no serif display, no terracotta essay column. The contract says diagrams
are the architecture rather than guests in the prose, and the build now agrees:
the vocabulary is complete at eleven diagram types and all eleven are in use,
across 61 numbered plates on 54 of the 60 lesson pages. The six pages without one
already carry a checklist or comparison table doing the same job; a plate is
added where a visual carries the idea better than the paragraph, never to close a
gap in a count.

**Key Characteristics:**

- White sheet, printing-ink hairlines, zero radius, zero blur.
- Elevation is a ruled line — 3px heavy, 1px ink, 1px soft.
- Seven module inks as page-scale fields carrying `#ffffff` type.
- Furniture in 0.6rem Helvetica caps at 0.11–0.18em tracking; running text in Charter at 64ch.
- Every figure is a numbered plate inside a ruled frame.
- Interactive furniture inverts to solid ink on hover in 0.12s.
- System font stacks only, no external request of any kind, works in print.

## Colors

A white sheet, one near-black printing ink, and seven saturated inks used as
fields — the only saturation on any page comes from a module identity or a
status.

### Primary

- **Printing Ink** (`#14141c`): every hairline, every rule, all body text, and
  the background of every inverted hover state. It is the same value as the rule
  token, on purpose: the line and the letter are one ink. 18.3:1 on the sheet.
- **Signal Red** (`#c1272d`): the academy's own accent, used only where nothing
  else carries identity — kickers, plate numbers, the reading-position bar, the
  focus ring, the note callout's top rule. Each course overrides it with its own
  ink, so this value is visible mostly on the landing page and in generic
  furniture.

### Secondary — the seven module inks

Each is a whole-panel field with white type: the route rail's module block, the
curriculum module header, the map node, the pill in the spec strip, the plate's
top rule, the progress fill.

- **Module 1 — Ultramarine** (`#2b3a8f`): module 1 in both courses; also the
  Software Sense course accent and the landing page's specimen field.
- **Module 2 — Teal** (`#0d6e8c`): module 2; also the tip / "Ask your AI" callout ink.
- **Module 3 — Forest** (`#157347`): module 3; also the pass/complete status ink.
- **Module 4 — Rust** (`#a8480c`): module 4; also The Agentic Coding Course accent.
- **Module 5 — Magenta** (`#a82a68`): module 5.
- **Module 6 — Violet** (`#5f3a9c`): module 6.
- **Module 7 — Vermilion** (`#b8332a`): module 7; also the warn/fail ink.

### Tertiary — status

- **Pass Green** (`#157347`) and **Fail Red** (`#b8332a`): completion, quiz
  correctness, red-flag callouts, and the ✓/✗/◐ glyphs in comparison figures.
  Both are borrowed from the module palette rather than added to it.

### Neutral

- **Sheet White** (`#ffffff`): the page. Also the type colour on every ink field.
- **Sheet Tint** (`#f2f1ec`): the recessed surface — callouts, code, the quiz
  block, the map ground, every hover row. It is a tint of paper, not a card.
- **Sheet Tint Deep** (`#e7e5dc`): one step further in, used only for the scored
  quiz option's hover.
- **Ink Secondary** (`#4c4c5a`): deks, captions, secondary furniture, table
  headings. 8.4:1 on the sheet.
- **Ink Furniture** (`#6d6d7c`): the smallest caps labels — spec keys, plate
  numbers, table captions, rail section heads. This is the floor of the palette.
- **Rule Soft** (`#c8c6bd`): the interior hairline and the resting underline on
  every link. Never carries type.
- **Edge** (`#807e75` light / `#6b6e80` dark): the one hairline that carries
  *meaning* rather than structure — the knowledge map's connections between
  lessons. Held above 3:1 on the plate ground, because a graphic that encodes a
  relationship is not decoration. Everything else structural uses Rule Soft.
- **Press Black** (`#000000`) and **Press White** (`#ffffff`): print only, inside
  `@media print`. On paper the whole palette collapses to two inks — every field
  becomes white with a black keyline, every tint becomes white. This is the one
  place in the system where pure black is correct; on screen the ink is
  `#14141c`.

### Named Rules

**The Field Rule.** A module ink appears one of two ways: as a page-scale field
with `#ffffff` type on it, or as type on the white sheet through its `t` variant.
It is never a small tinted chip behind dark text. The one licensed exception is a
wash — `color-mix(in srgb, <ink> 10–22%, var(--paper))` under body ink, used for
the loaded quadrant of a 2×2 and the shaded band of a scale. A wash is a region;
a chip is a badge. Badges are the rejected world.

**The Two-Way AA Rule.** An ink joins the palette only if it is legible in both
directions against the sheet: white type on the field, and the ink itself as type
on white. Every current module ink measures ≥5.79:1 against `#ffffff` (min:
Teal at 5.79, max: Ultramarine at 9.98), and every dark-theme `t` variant
measures ≥7.09:1 against `#0f1017`. This replaced a pastel-pill palette that
failed; it is load-bearing, not taste.

**The Furniture Floor Rule.** Ink Furniture on Sheet Tint measures exactly
4.50:1 — the AA minimum for text under 18.66px, which is all of it. That pair is
the floor of the system. Do not lighten Ink Furniture, do not darken Sheet Tint,
and do not introduce a third grey between them.

**The Borrowed Semantics Rule.** Pass Green is module ink 3, Fail Red is module
ink 7, and in dark theme the academy accent and the fail ink are the same value.
Hue therefore never carries pass/fail on its own — the caps label
(`RED FLAGS TO CATCH`, `✓ COMPLETE`) is what says it. Never build a state that is
distinguishable only by colour.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Helvetica Neue / Helvetica / Arial / Liberation Sans / Nimbus Sans (system grotesque)
**Body Font:** Charter / Bitstream Charter / Sitka Text / Iowan Old Style / Palatino / Georgia (system serif)
**Label/Mono Font:** SF Mono / Cascadia Mono / DejaVu Sans Mono / Menlo / Consolas

**Character:** A Swiss grotesque does all the shouting and all the labelling; a
sturdy transitional serif does all the reading. The grotesque is set tight and
loud — negative tracking that deepens as the size grows, weight 700 almost
everywhere. The serif is set at a generous 1.6 line-height and held to a 64ch
measure. The pairing reads as a technical document, not as an essay.

### Hierarchy

- **Display** (700, `clamp(5rem, 15vw, 8.5rem)`, 0.78, −0.06em): one enormous
  numeral on the landing page's specimen field — the count of lessons. One per
  site.
- **Display SM** (700, `clamp(3.4rem, 9vw, 6.2rem)`, 0.8, −0.055em): the same
  device at course scale, in the monograph head's ink field.
- **Headline** (700, `clamp(2.1rem, 1.5rem + 2.6vw, 3.1rem)`, 1.02, −0.028em):
  the page's `h1`. Balanced wrapping, ≤26ch on the monograph head.
- **Title** (700, `clamp(1.35rem, 1.2rem + 0.7vw, 1.65rem)`, 1.12): every `h2`.
  It always carries a 1px ink rule above it with 0.65rem of clearance — the
  section break is a printed rule, not whitespace.
- **Subhead** (700, `1.06rem`, 1.25, −0.015em): `h3`. Inside a card or column it
  goes uppercase at 0.06–0.09em tracking.
- **Dek** (400, `clamp(1.02rem, 0.98rem + 0.35vw, 1.18rem)`, 1.4, −0.014em): the
  one place the grotesque is set at weight 400 — the standfirst under every `h1`,
  ≤54ch.
- **Body** (400, `clamp(1rem, 0.96rem + 0.2vw, 1.075rem)`, 1.6): Charter, held
  to 64ch. `text-wrap: pretty`.
- **Label** (700, `0.6rem`, 0.14em, uppercase): all furniture — spec keys, plate
  numbers, table headings and captions, callout labels, prev/next kickers, rail
  section heads. The single most repeated type object in the system.
- **Kicker** (700, `0.68rem`, 0.18em, uppercase, accent-coloured): the line above
  an `h1` that names the angle. The loosest tracking in the system.
- **Action** (700, `0.79rem`, 0.12em, uppercase) / **Action SM** (700, `0.72rem`,
  0.11em, uppercase): buttons and links-as-buttons.
- **Data** (700, `1.35rem`, 1, −0.035em): a measured value under its caps key —
  the spec strip's numbers, a figure's headline figure.
- **Data LG** (700, `1.5rem`, 1.05, −0.035em): the one big phrase inside a
  comparison panel (`.dgm-vs .big`) — a value the reader is meant to compare, not
  read.
- **Headline LG** (700, `clamp(2.2rem, 1.3rem + 3.4vw, 3.5rem)`): the landing
  page's `h1` only. It is the one line that outranks a course `h1`.
- **Title LG** (700, `clamp(1.3rem, 1.1rem + 0.8vw, 1.7rem)`, 1.05, −0.03em): a
  course name on its ink field — the landing cards and nothing else.
- **Display XS** (700, `2.6rem`, 0.85, −0.05em): the route rail's module numeral.
  The Display family's fourth and smallest step.
- **Fact** (700, `clamp(2.4rem, 7vw, 3.6rem)`, 0.85, −0.055em): one hard number
  standing alone inside a figure (`.dgm-fact`) — the Display device brought down
  to figure scale.
- **Interface** (400–700, `0.87–0.95rem`, 1.35): the size at which a control
  speaks — quiz options, the search box, card body, note and tip bodies. Grotesque,
  sentence case. The one tier that is *not* furniture and *not* running text.
- **Note** (400–700, `0.735–0.79rem`, 1.35–1.45): the smallest sentence-case
  grotesque in the system — a figure's note and its inner text, margin notes,
  table cells, search results, rail TOC entries, the `.plate-h` help line.
- **Map furniture** (700, `9.5–13px` inside the SVG; `15–18px` below 640px):
  node numbers, node labels, module titles, module counters. Absolute pixels
  because the map's viewBox rescales, so `rem` would not hold. The mobile
  override exists because the viewBox shrinks hard on a phone.
- **Mono** (`0.83rem` block / `0.84em` inline, 1.55): code only, on Sheet Tint
  inside a hairline.

### Named Rules

**The Two Voices Rule.** Charter sets running text. The grotesque sets everything
that is furniture — labels, spec strips, plate numbers, tables, captions, deks,
headings, buttons, the whole route rail, and all text inside a figure. There is
no third voice except code. If you cannot say which of the two a new element is,
it is furniture.

**The Caps Furniture Rule.** Furniture is 0.585–0.68rem, weight 700, uppercase,
tracked 0.11–0.18em. At that size tighter tracking prints as a smudge and looser
prints as a logo. Never set furniture in lowercase and never below 0.585rem.

**The Tightening Rule.** Negative tracking scales with size: −0.014em at dek,
−0.028em at `h1`/`h2`, −0.035em at a data value, −0.06em at the specimen
numeral. No grotesque display line is set at normal tracking.

**The System Stack Rule.** System font stacks only. No webfont, no
`@font-face`, no external request — not now, not for one page. The fallback
chains are the design.

## Layout

**The sheet.** Prose pages use a single column capped at `64ch + 6rem` with
`clamp(1.1rem, 4vw, 2rem)` side padding. Index pages that carry grids
(curriculum, resources) use a 78rem container, but their own prose children —
paragraphs, deks, lists, callouts — are individually re-capped to the 64ch
measure, so a wide page never produces a long line.

**The lesson sheet is three tracks.** A 16rem sticky route rail, the text
column, and a 17rem figure gutter to its right, inside a 96rem outer frame. The
vertical rule beside the rail is painted as a `linear-gradient` on the grid
container rather than as a border on the rail, so it runs the full height of the
page instead of stopping where the sticky rail's content ends. Prose in the main
column holds `min(64ch, 100%)`; only figures, the spec strip, and `.wide`
elements are allowed to cross into the gutter. Margin notes may float into the
gutter above 1180px.

**Full-bleed frames.** The landing specimen, the monograph head, the map plate,
and the course cards are all 96rem-wide frames closed by a 3px rule at the
bottom. They sit outside the reading container, edge to edge.

**Grids are made of rules, not gaps.** A ruled panel group sets the container's
background to the rule ink and its `gap` to 1px; the children's own backgrounds
then leave exactly one hairline between them. Applied to `.cards`, `.actions`,
`.prevnext`, `.legend`, `.home-cols`, `.cc-actions`, and the diagram stack. The
one deliberate exception is the curriculum lesson grid, which puts the hairlines
on the cards instead: a background grid leaves grey voids in the last row
whenever a module's lesson count doesn't fill it.

**Vertical rhythm.** 1.8rem between blocks (tables, cards, quizzes, step lists),
1.9rem for callouts, 2.4rem for a figure plate and for the spec strip, 3rem for
a horizontal rule, 3.4rem above an `h2`. Cell padding sits in a 0.45–0.7rem band
for furniture cells and a 1rem–1.2rem band for panels.

### Named Rules

**The Measure Rule.** Running text holds 64ch. A page may be as wide as it needs
to be; a line may not. Widening a container is never a reason to widen prose.

**The Local Breakpoint Rule.** There is no global breakpoint ladder. Each
component collapses at the width its own content actually breaks — 520/560/620px
for diagrams and furniture, 640/680px for the map and step chains, 760/820px for
the two head blocks, 1080px where the lesson rail becomes a horizontal strip,
1180px where the figure gutter opens. Do not invent a shared `sm/md/lg` scale to
tidy this up; the numbers are content measurements.

## Elevation & Depth

There are no shadows in this system — not one blurred shadow exists in either
stylesheet, and none may be added. Depth is expressed entirely as a ruled line,
in a three-step hierarchy: a **3px heavy rule** opens a block and says "a new
object starts here"; a **1px ink hairline** frames and divides objects inside it;
a **1px soft hairline** divides rows within an object. Recession is a flat tint
(Sheet Tint), never a lift. A sticky top navigation is separated from the page by
a 3px rule, not by a shadow.

`box-shadow` is nonetheless used, and legitimately: it is the only way to draw
certain rules that a border cannot. Every instance has a blur radius of `0` and
functions as a drawn line.

### Shadow Vocabulary

- **Inset state bar** (`box-shadow: inset 3px 0 0 <status ink>`): a 3px vertical
  bar inside the left edge of a quiz option, marking correct or wrong without
  changing the option's box size.
- **Inset current-page underline** (`box-shadow: inset 0 -3px 0 <accent>`): the
  active-page marker inside the sticky nav, where a border would shift the row.
- **Inset focus fill** (`box-shadow: inset 0 0 0 3px <accent>`): the search
  field's focus treatment, replacing the default outline inside the field.
- **Shared cell outline** (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px <rule>` / `0 0 0 3px <ink>`):
  the 2×2 matrix's cell seams. Adjacent cells' outlines overlap exactly, so every
  seam is one hairline wide with no per-cell border arithmetic.

### Named Rules

**The Ruled-Line Rule.** Elevation is a ruled line. If something needs to read as
raised, separate, or new, give it a heavier rule — never a shadow, never a
gradient, never a lift on hover.

**The Zero-Blur Rule.** `box-shadow` is permitted only with a blur radius of
exactly 0, as a drawn rule that a border cannot produce. Any blur value, any
`filter: drop-shadow`, any spread meant to read as glow belongs to the world this
one refuses.

**The Print Fallback Rule.** Every colour field must still read when its
background is dropped. In print the whole token set re-declares to black on white
and every field — pill, course card head, module header, specimen block, rail
block — becomes a white box with a 1px black border. A new field-carrying
component is not finished until it has a print rule.

## Shapes

Zero radius, everywhere, without exception. `border-radius` is not set once in
either stylesheet, and the form language depends on it: the knowledge map draws
lessons as 19px squares and module hubs as 15px squares rather than circles,
because a dot is a different world's mark. Progress is a rectangular track with a
1px ink border and a flat fill — a sheet measures, it does not gauge.

Every object is a rectangle bounded by rules. Frames close with a 3px rule on the
side that opens the object (the top of a callout, figure, spec strip, quiz, or
prev/next block; the bottom of a full-bleed head). Interiors are subdivided by
hairlines. Arrows in diagrams are built from CSS borders — a 1px line plus a
0.3rem transparent-border triangle — so they inherit the ink and reverse
correctly in dark theme and print. Selection, focus, and completion are all
marked by rectangles: a 3px accent outline offset 2px for focus, an 11px square
stamped on a map node's corner for completion.

## Components

### Buttons

- **Shape:** hard rectangle, no radius, no border on filled variants.
- **CTA (primary):** the page's ink field with `#ffffff` caps, 0.75rem 1.3rem,
  0.79rem/0.12em uppercase. On hover it brightens by `filter: brightness(1.15)`
  rather than changing colour — the field keeps its identity.
- **CTA secondary:** white sheet with ink type; on hover it inverts to a solid
  ink block with white type.
- **Row button (`.btn`):** used inside a ruled action row where 1px gaps are the
  separators. Flat white, ink caps at 0.72rem/0.11em; the primary one in the row
  takes the course accent as a field. A ghost variant drops the type to Ink
  Secondary.
- **Mark-complete button:** full-width, white with a 3px ink rule on top; hover
  inverts to solid ink. Once complete, the rule and the type switch to Pass Green
  and hover fills with Pass Green — the only component whose colour identity
  changes with state.
- **Focus:** a 3px Signal Red outline offset 2px, globally, on every focusable
  element. Never removed, only replaced (the search field replaces it with an
  inset fill of the same weight).

### Chips

- **Pill (module tag):** the module ink as a solid field with `#ffffff` caps at
  0.635rem/0.11em, padding 0.28rem 0.55rem 0.26rem, zero radius. It is a stamped
  field, not a badge. There is no outlined or tinted variant.

### Cards / Containers

- **Corner style:** square. No radius.
- **Ruled panel (`.card`):** 1rem 1.15rem 1.1rem on the white sheet, separated
  from its siblings by a 1px hairline supplied by the parent grid, with the whole
  group framed by a hairline. It is a panel in a table, not a floating card.
- **Callout (`.note` / `.tip` / `.warn`):** Sheet Tint (or plain sheet, for
  `warn`) inside a soft hairline, opened by a 3px rule whose colour names the
  kind — accent for a note, Teal for a tip or "Ask your AI", Fail Red for a
  warning — and repeated in a 0.615rem/0.15em caps label on the first line. The
  label is required; the rule colour alone never carries the meaning.
- **Shadow strategy:** none. See Elevation & Depth.

### Inputs / Fields

- **Search box:** full width, white, hairline border with a 3px rule on top,
  0.65rem 0.85rem, grotesque at 0.9rem. Placeholder in Ink Furniture.
- **Focus:** the outline is replaced by `inset 0 0 0 3px` accent, so the field
  thickens inward instead of growing.
- **Results:** a hairline-divided list hanging directly off the field with no top
  border of its own, each row inverting to solid ink on hover.

### Navigation

- **Top bar:** sticky, white, closed by a 3px ink rule; links are cells divided
  by soft hairlines, set in the grotesque at 0.755rem/0.03em weight 600. Hover
  inverts the cell to solid ink. The current page takes a Sheet Tint background
  plus a 3px inset accent underline. On the right: an optional progress chip
  ("12 / 30 done", the count in Pass Green) and a 2.9rem theme toggle.
- **Mobile (≤620px):** the bar becomes horizontally scrollable with the
  scrollbar hidden, the progress chip is dropped, the "Academy" label shortens to
  its arrow, and the theme toggle is pinned `position: sticky; right: 0` so it
  stays reachable at the edge.
- **Prev / next:** a two-cell ruled block at the foot of every lesson, opened by
  a 3px rule, each cell a caps kicker over the lesson title, inverting to ink on
  hover. A lone neighbour spans both columns.

### The Specification Strip (signature)

The object that answers "where am I" for a reader who arrived mid-course from a
search result. A `dl` rendered as a 4-column data table — `1.7fr` for the module,
then three equal columns — framed by a hairline with a 3px rule on top, keys in
0.6rem/0.14em caps in Ink Furniture, values in 0.83rem weight 600. It carries
Module (as a pill), Lesson *n* of 30, reading time computed from the page's own
word count, and completion status, which turns Pass Green when marked. Collapses
to two columns at 620px. It sits above the kicker and `h1`, and appears on all
60 lesson pages.

### The Route Rail (signature)

A 16rem sticky column standing in for the docs-site sidebar tree it refuses. Top
to bottom: the module's ink field carrying a 2.6rem module number and the module
title in white; a caps "On this page" label; a section list built at runtime from
the page's own `h2` elements with scroll-spy marking the current one via a 3px
left border in the module ink; then a footer with an *n*/*m* module count, a 6px
rectangular progress track filled in the module ink, and a jump link that
becomes the module quiz once the module is complete. Below 1080px the whole rail
becomes a horizontal strip above the text: the ink field goes inline, the section
list becomes a scrollable row of tabs with the current one underlined by a 3px
bottom border, and the progress bar sits in a flex row.

### The Numbered Plate (signature)

Every figure is a plate: a `figure` framed by a hairline and opened by a 3px rule
in the figure's own ink, containing a caption row whose `::before` auto-numbers
it "Fig. *n*" from a body-level counter, a padded body, and an optional note
under a soft hairline. Any diagram accepts a `--f` custom property to set its
ink, defaulting to the page's module field. The vocabulary is eleven flat
HTML/CSS diagram types — layer stack, step flow (with a dashed return rail for a
loop), two-column comparison, 2×2 matrix, proportion bars, marked scale, decision
tree, numbered sequence, anatomy callouts, comparison matrix, and a lone big
fact. No SVG, no JS, no images.

### The Knowledge Map Plate (signature)

A pannable, zoomable SVG on a Sheet Tint ground inside a ruled plate frame, with
a caps plate number and title above it and a hairline-divided module legend
below. Modules sit on a ring as small ink squares with a ruled progress bar
beneath each; lessons are 19px ink squares carrying their number in white, with
labels alternating above and below to avoid collision and a white paint-order
stroke so text stays readable over edges. Edges are 1px soft-hairline curves.
Highlighting works by dimming everything else to 12% opacity over 0.18s, never by
brightening the subject. The first uncompleted lesson gets a 2.4s pulsing square
outline. The legend doubles as a keyboard-operable filter with `aria-pressed`,
and pinned modules clear on Escape.

### Named Rules

**The Inversion Rule.** Interactive furniture inverts on hover: background
becomes Printing Ink, text becomes Sheet White, over 0.12s. That single gesture
covers nav links, buttons, search results, prev/next cells, table-of-contents
entries, quiz options, map controls, and legend items. Never a tint, never a
lift, never a radius, never a colour change to something new.

**The Field-Follows-Page Rule.** `--field` on the `body` element is the only
per-page identity declaration, and every accented component reads it with a
fallback (`var(--field, var(--ink))`). Setting it once recolours the rail block,
the module pill, the plate rules, the progress fills, the step numerals, the
diagram inks, and the current-section marker together. Figures may override with
`--f`; landing course cards use `--course-accent`. Never hard-code a module hex
into a component.

**The Text-Variant Rule.** When a module ink is used as a *field*, use the base
ink (`--m1`…`--m7`). When it is used as *type* or as a thin stroke on the sheet,
use the text variant (`--m1t`…`--m7t`), which is identical in light theme and
re-tinted lighter in dark theme. A base ink used as type goes muddy on the dark
sheet.

**The One Authored Moment Rule.** Motion is one gesture: a colour field wipes
down from `clip-path: inset(0 0 100% 0)` with `animation-fill-mode: backwards`,
0.5–0.62s on `cubic-bezier(.16, 1, .3, 1)`, staggered 0.14s and 0.24s for the two
course cards. Content is never animated and is visible on the first frame, so
nothing can be left invisible if the animation never runs. State changes are
0.12s. The progress bar animates `transform: scaleX()` rather than `width`,
because it re-runs on every mark-complete click. Everything is disabled under
`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:

- **Do** put a module ink on screen as a page-scale field with `#ffffff` type, or
  as type through its `--mNt` variant. Nothing in between.
- **Do** open every new block with a 3px rule and divide its interior with 1px
  hairlines: heavy rule → ink hairline → soft hairline.
- **Do** read the page's identity from `var(--field, var(--ink))` (or `--f` in a
  figure, `--course-accent` on a landing card) so one `body` declaration recolours
  the whole page.
- **Do** set every label, key, caption, plate number, table heading, and button in
  the grotesque at 0.585–0.68rem, weight 700, uppercase, tracked 0.11–0.18em.
- **Do** hold running text to 64ch, whatever the container width, and re-cap prose
  children individually on wide index pages.
- **Do** invert interactive furniture to solid ink on hover in 0.12s.
- **Do** wrap every figure in a `.fig` plate so it gets a number, a caption, and a
  rule in the page's ink.
- **Do** check a new ink both ways against the sheet — ≥4.5:1 for white type on it
  *and* for it as type on white — plus its dark-theme variant against `#0f1017`.
- **Do** give every field-carrying component a print rule that drops the
  background to white and outlines the box in 1px black.
- **Do** pair every status colour with a caps label that says the same thing.

### Don't:

- **Don't** set `border-radius` anywhere. Not on a button, not on an input, not on
  a map node. The value is 0 and it is the world.
- **Don't** use a blurred shadow, a `drop-shadow` filter, a gradient meant to read
  as depth, or a hover lift. `box-shadow` is allowed only at blur 0, as a drawn
  rule.
- **Don't** render a module ink as a small pastel chip behind dark text. A 10–22%
  `color-mix` wash under body ink across a region is the only tinted use.
- **Don't** add a webfont, an icon font, a CDN stylesheet, or any external asset
  request. System stacks and inline SVG only.
- **Don't** set body copy in the grotesque, or furniture in the serif. There is no
  third family except monospace for code.
- **Don't** lighten Ink Furniture or darken Sheet Tint — that pair already sits
  exactly at 4.50:1, the AA floor.
- **Don't** hard-code a module hex inside a component's CSS; read `--field`/`--f`.
- **Don't** distinguish any state by hue alone — Pass Green is also module ink 3,
  Fail Red is also module ink 7, and in dark theme the accent equals the fail ink.
- **Don't** animate content in or out. Only a colour field moves, and the text
  under it is on screen from frame one.
- **Don't** introduce a global `sm/md/lg` breakpoint scale. Breakpoints are
  per-component content measurements.
- **Don't** build a sidebar tree, a thin-sans docs shell, or blue underlined
  links; and don't reintroduce cream paper, a serif display face, or terracotta.
